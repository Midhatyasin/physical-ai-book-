## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Designing Safe Robot Systems (Priority: P1)

As a robotics engineer, I want to design safety into my robot from the start, so that it operates without harming humans.

**Why this priority**: Safety is paramount for physical AI systems.

**Independent Test**: Reader can perform risk assessment and implement safety systems.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a reader performs risk assessment, **When** they identify hazards, **Then** they can mitigate them.
2. **Given** a reader implements safety systems, **When** dangerous conditions arise, **Then** the robot responds safely.
3. **Given** a reader understands standards, **When** they design systems, **Then** they meet ISO 10218 requirements.

---

### User Story 2 - Understanding AI Ethics (Priority: P2)

As a technologist, I want to understand the ethical implications of physical AI, so that I can develop responsible technology.

**Why this priority**: Ethics ensures beneficial deployment of AI.

**Independent Test**: Reader can identify ethical issues and propose mitigations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a reader completes the ethics section, **When** they analyze scenarios, **Then** they identify bias and fairness issues.
2. **Given** a reader understands privacy, **When** they design systems, **Then** they protect user data.
3. **Given** a reader considers transparency, **When** they explain robot decisions, **Then** reasoning is clear.
